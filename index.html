📖 Quick reference

  

    WD lookup
    
SAM.gov → Wage Determinations → state → county → trade. For Davis-Bacon, the WD gives a base rate and (usually) a cash fringe rate. We almost always use 4(b) because the cash fringe is paid as a separate QBO line item.

  

  

    Daily hours distribution
    
If the stub gives a weekly DB total and you have a timecard, use the timecard. If not: distribute proportionally across the DB days, rounding to 2 decimals, last day absorbs the remainder. Sum of 7 day cells must equal the weekly DB total exactly.

  

  

    Deduction pro-rating
    
ratio = db_wages / stub_total_wages. db_wages = base × db_hours + fringe × db_hours. Each stub deduction (FICA, W/H, Other) is multiplied by ratio and rounded to 2 decimals. Net = base gross − total_ded. Math must hold to the cent for every row.

  

  

    Exclusions from the WH-347
    
Per diem, training, bonuses, drive time are not DB wages — exclude from gross, deductions, and net. They appear on the stub but not on the form.

  

  

    QBO PDF gotcha
    
"Print to PDF" / "Save as PDF" truncates the long line list around 6-7 lines and hides the DB / fringe / per-diem rows. Always parse from the on-screen view or an email-sent copy.
